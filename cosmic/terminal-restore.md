Run `maprisma-theme-helper terminal-restore` to restore the COSMIC Terminal dark and light syntax theme selections saved before the first `terminal` command and remove Maprisma color schemes. User schemes are preserved. The helper saves the selections once in `$XDG_STATE_HOME/maprisma-theme/terminal-previous.json` (default `~/.local/state/maprisma-theme/terminal-previous.json`), then removes the backup after a successful restore. A missing selection is restored by removing its override. Repeating restore without a backup is harmless.
